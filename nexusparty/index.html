How the fallback works

  1. Discover — joining a room tries engines in ranked order until it finds the one where the room lives (each provider is its own namespace — no central registry needed).
  2. Stay free — ≤ 8 seats defaults to P2P mesh (serverless, unmetered). Bigger rooms need an SFU: Jitsi public, your Daily free room, or your self-hosted Jitsi.
  3. Self-heal — an engine failure opens its circuit breaker, the room re-routes to the next free provider, and you reconnect automatically.
